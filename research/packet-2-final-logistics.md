## Ground Rules at Ross Relles

- **Afternoon free time:** Kids must be outside, in lodge, or on porch — no cabins unless a parent is there or they have permission from Kelsey
- **Stick to boundaries:** Kids outside must keep an adult in sight at all times. It's up to the kids to do this.
- Come right to lodge when called
- No running or rough housing in lodge
- Only use the slide with an adult present. Stick to our school slide rules.
- Clean up after yourself in lodge, bathrooms, and cabins
- Girls can't go to boys' cabin area and boys can't go into girls' cabin area
- No food in cabins
- **Quiet hours: 9:15 pm – 7:00 am.** If up before 7:00, read, draw, or daydream — don't wake others.
- No going into the lodge before 7:15 am without an adult
- Be respectful of facilities and equipment — we are guests here!
- Mining groups are assigned to clean up after one meal: clear and wash down tables, sweep, push in chairs
- Be kind and respectful — no excluding or teasing of any kind
- Ask an adult for help if you need it
- Have fun; be kind. Tell an adult / Kelsey if you are feeling upset or uncomfortable about anything.
